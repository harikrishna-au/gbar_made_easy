## Delivery notes (Tinglish-specific)

**Code-switch, don't translate.** These stay English because students say them in English, and translating them makes the VO sound dubbed: *timer, score, matrix, communication round, cognitive, read aloud, meeting link, slot confirm, premium, practice*. The Telugu carries the feeling; the English carries the machinery.

**Vowel length matters.** "padi nimishallo" = ten minutes. "padhi" also ten — keep it short and clipped, it's a beat, not a number being announced. "Rendu vandala mandi" should land flat and factual, not dramatic.

**Don't oversell the Telugu.** Two or three Telugu words per line is the campus register. Full Telugu sentences in a reveal video will feel like a regional ad, not a founder talking.

**The turn is the only place to slow down.** "Kani aa room lo kurchoni feel ento telidu" — one clean pause after it. If the whole script is flat, this is the line that either earns the reveal or loses it.

**Record a human, not TTS.** Telugu-English code-switching gets mangled by every TTS engine — the Telugu comes out in a different accent than the English and the whole thing sounds like a translation. This needs a real voice, ideally the founder's own.

## Verify before you record

Same list as the English cut:
- "Rendu vandala mandi" — your actual hall, your actual number
- "Moodu years back pass aina seniors" — keep only if that's really what you were watching
- "Ekkada aagipoyaro exact ga chepthundi" — confirm the speaking feedback actually localises
- The build beat is thin — if your first version really broke, put the real failure in. It's the most starter-story sentence in the script.

**Do not add:** placement rates, student counts, company "partnership", or a quote. No approved evidence exists for any of them, and `PRODUCT.md` rules them out.
