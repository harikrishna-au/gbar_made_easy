# Harry The Blaze — Product Reveal Script

**Format:** founder-story reveal ("starter story" shape). Problem → what the round actually is → what I built → the turn → reveal → close.
**Length:** 1:35 main cut · 0:30 cutdown · 0:06 end card
**Voice:** first person, campus-plain, ~145 wpm, Indian English, warm and unhurried. No hype adjectives.
**Canvas:** 1920×1080 primary. 1080×1920 cutdown uses the same VO, re-cropped.

---

## The one line

> Nobody explains the first round. So I built it — then added the person who just cleared it.

---

## Main cut — 1:35

### 1. Hook — 0:00–0:11
**VO:** Final year. One company on the calendar. Two hundred of us in one hall, and nobody could tell us what the first round actually looked like.

**On-screen:** `FINAL YEAR.` → `ONE COMPANY.` → `NOBODY EXPLAINS THE FIRST ROUND.`
**Visual:** A crowded exam hall, shot from the back. Holds still. Text lands over it, no motion flourish.

---

### 2. The wrong prep — 0:11–0:23
**VO:** So we prepped the wrong thing. Aptitude PDFs. A list of HR questions downloaded off a forum. Videos from seniors who graduated three years ago, describing a process that had already changed.

**On-screen:** `WRONG MATERIAL.` `RIGHT FEAR.`
**Visual:** Screen recording of a phone: twelve browser tabs, a PDF, a YouTube playlist. The tab counter is the joke — let it get absurd.

---

### 3. What the round actually is — 0:23–0:43
**VO:** Then we found out the round was games. Balloon math on a timer. A matrix that shifts while you're still looking at it. And a communication round that asks you to read aloud, repeat a sentence, catch a mistake, and write an email in ten minutes.

**On-screen:** `BALLOON MATH` · `MATRIX FLOW` · `HIDDEN MAZE` · `READ ALOUD` · `REPEAT` · `ERROR CORRECTION` · `WRITTEN EMAIL`
**Visual:** Real product captures, one per name, cut on the word. This is the first time the product appears — keep it fast and factual, no demo voiceover yet.

---

### 4. What I built — 0:43–0:56
**VO:** So I built the version I wish I'd had. Same games. Same timer. You speak into it, it scores you, and it tells you exactly where you went quiet.

**On-screen:** `SAME GAMES. SAME TIMER.`
**Visual:** Matrix Flow running, timer ticking. Cut to the speaking round: waveform, then the feedback line appearing beside it. The wave going flat is the money shot — hold on it.

---

### 5. The turn — 0:56–1:14
**VO:** But practice has a ceiling. You can learn the round. You still don't know what it felt like to sit in it. So the second half of this is a person — twenty minutes, one-to-one, with a senior who cleared these exact rounds this year.

**On-screen:** `YOU CAN LEARN THE ROUND.` → `YOU CAN'T LEARN THE ROOM.`
**Visual:** A laptop closed. Then the Connect page: senior cards, a slot being picked, a payment confirmation, one meeting link. Slow down here — this is a live paid offer, and calm reads as real.

---

### 6. Reveal — 1:14–1:29
**VO:** This is Harry The Blaze. Practice the Accenture 2026 cognitive and communication rounds whenever you want. Then book Connect, and ask the things no video answers. Premium practice is ₹299. The call is paid per booking — we confirm the slot and send one meeting link to both of you.

**On-screen:** wordmark lockup → `PRACTICE` / `CONNECT 1:1` → `₹299` → `ONE LINK. BOTH OF YOU.`
**Visual:** Clean stone-canvas card. The two offers sit side by side, equally visible. No badge wall, no logo strip, no testimonial.

---

### 7. Close — 1:29–1:35
**VO:** You don't need another playlist. You need the round, and someone who just did it.

**On-screen:** `harrytheblaze.site` — `Free account. Premium ₹299.`

## Production notes

**Look.** Stone-and-ink on `#fcfcf9`. Merriweather for display, Inter for UI. Accent is the warm champagne already in the app (`rgba(201,164,110,...)`) and only on one hairline. No purple gradient, no amber glow, no neon — that's a pinned brand rule, not a preference.

**Motion.** Cuts and type alone until 0:23. Nothing animates before the product does. From 0:23, motion is only ever the product moving.

**Music.** One bed, soft and low, no drop. Let it breathe out for one beat under "You can't learn the room." Then nothing under the reveal VO except room tone — the price needs to land in silence.

**Captions.** Burned-in, sans, bottom-safe. Assume it plays muted in a feed.

**What to capture before editing.**
1. Matrix Flow, Balloon Math, Hidden Maze each mid-round with the timer visible
2. The speaking round: waveform live, then the scored feedback
3. Dashboard with a real (or demo-seeded) progress state
4. Connect: senior cards → slot picker → payment screen → confirmation email
5. One clean shot of the landing page on a phone

## Verify before you record

These are narrative claims, not product facts — make them true or cut them:

- "Two hundred of us in one hall" — your actual drive, your actual number
- "videos from seniors who graduated three years ago" — keep only if that's really what you were watching
- "it tells you exactly where you went quiet" — confirm the speaking feedback actually localises
- The build beat is deliberately thin — if you have a real first-version failure (a timer that scored people who hadn't started, a broken microphone pass), put it in. That's the most starter-story sentence in the script.

**Do not add:** placement rates, student counts, company "partnership", or a customer quote. There's no approved evidence for any of them, and PRODUCT.md rules them out.
