# Harry The Blaze — 5-Minute Solo Script (Starter Story grammar)

**Format:** one person on camera, no host, no guest — but built on Starter Story's actual skeleton: cold open with a number → the big idea → who I am → the problem → v1 and what broke → validation → the turn → how it works → stack → costs → what I'd do differently → final word.
**Difference from the last draft:** that one was a film. This one is a *breakdown* — every chapter has a number, a decision, or a mistake in it.
**Voice:** the founder, alone, talking to camera. Conversational, specific, no cinematic pauses.
**Supers:** English. Voice: Tinglish.
**Runtime:** 5:15

---

## The big idea

> **Software can teach the round. It can't teach the room.**
> So I charged for the part I couldn't automate.

That's the whole episode. Every chapter either sets up that gap or proves it.

## Script

### 00:00 — Intro

**VO:**
> Nenu ee product ni okkadine build chesa. Final year lo, hostel room lo.
> Padakondu nelalu. 343 commits. Roju two hours, exams unna rojulu kuda.
> Anduku enduku? Rendu vandala mandi unna hall lo, okka round ela untundo evariki telidu. So nenu aa round ni build chesa.

**Supers:** `ONE PERSON` · `11 MONTHS` · `343 COMMITS`
**Visual:** You at the desk, plain white wall. Three numbers count up in the first four seconds. No music sting.

---

### 00:22 — The big idea

**VO:**
> Ee video lo naa daggara oka idea undi, adi chepthanu.
> Software round ni nerpistundi. Kani room ni nerpinchaledu.
> Aa gap ne naa product lo rendo bhagam ayyindi. Inka adi ne naa business model.

**Super:** `SOFTWARE TEACHES THE ROUND.` / `IT CAN'T TEACH THE ROOM.`
**Visual:** Full-frame text card. Two lines only. Hold 3 seconds — let it be the thesis before anything else.

---

### 00:40 — Who I am

**VO:**
> Nenu [BRANCH] student, [COLLEGE]. Naa batch lo [N] mandi.
> Nenu topper kaadu. Coding bane telusu, aptitude average.
> Anduke nenu build cheyagalanu. Kani first version ni chala wrong ga build chesa — adi ippudu chepthanu.

**Super:** `[BRANCH] · [COLLEGE]`
**Visual:** Straight to camera. No B-roll. This is the only chapter that's just your face.

---

### 01:05 — The round nobody explained

**VO:**
> Placement season lo oka company vastundi, oka date untundi, okate first round.
> Aa round aptitude exam kaadu. Adi games. Balloon math, timer tick avuthu. Matrix flow — pattern maaruthundi, meeru next step cheppali. Hidden maze — screen lo kanipinchadu.
> Ivi memory meeda, speed meeda, pressure meeda. Rote learning tho raadu.
> Taruvata communication round — read aloud, sentence repeat, error correction, written email. Enimidi round types.
> Ee round gurinchi online lo oka proper video ledu. Anduke andaru old PDFs tho prepare avutharu. Adi waste.

**Supers:** `3 GAMES` · `8 ROUND TYPES` · `0 GOOD VIDEOS`
**Visual:** Screen share — each game for two seconds, timer visible. Cut on the count. Then a quick shot of the PDFs you actually used.

---

### 01:40 — v1, and what broke

**VO:**
> Naaku write cheyyadam telusu, so web app chesa. Android kaadu — naa friends andaru phone browser ne open chestharu.
> First version: okka game, oka timer, oka score screen. Rendu gantalaki raatri, hostel room lo.
> [WHAT BROKE — one specific bug, one sentence.]
> Adi naaku gamaninchaledu. Naa friend gamaninchadu.
> Aa roju naa product direction maaripoindi. Features kaadu — realism.

**Super:** `v1`
**Visual:** Show the ugly version. Console open, broken layout, a 0 score. Do not clean this up.

---

### 02:20 — Validation, my batch

**VO:**
> Validation ki nenu peddaga em cheyyaledu. Naa batch lo [N] mandiki pampinchanu.
> Andulo [N] mandi actual ga aa round ki vellaru. Taruvata vachi chepparu: "idi game la undi, kani real round la ledu."
> Aa feedback ne naa roadmap ayyindi. Nenu features add cheyyaledu. Timer ni corner ki pampinchanu. Instructions ni shorten chesanu. Scoring ni strict chesanu.
> Product chinnaga maarindi. Kani real ga maarindi.

**Super:** `"IDI GAME LA UNDI."`
**Visual:** The two timer positions side by side — centre vs corner. One visual argument, four seconds. This is the proof the product was built from feedback.

---

### 02:55 — The turn

**VO:**
> Taruvata oka vishayam gamaninchanu.
> Naa friend round clear ayyadu. Inko friend clear avvaledu. Iddari practice scores same. Same games, same material.
> Difference enti? Oka manishi aa room lo kurchoni em feel ayyado, aa tension ela handle chesado — adi naa software nerpinchaledu. Evaru nerpinchaledu.
> So nenu software ki leni bhagam add chesa: oka manishi. Iravai nimishalu, recently place ayina senior tho.
> Deenini nenu automate cheyyaledu. Enduku ante adi automatable kaadu. Anduke adi separate ga charge chestundi.

**Supers:** `SAME PREP.` → `DIFFERENT RESULT.`
**Visual:** Two score screens, identical numbers. Hold. Then cut to camera for "deenini nenu automate cheyyaledu."

---

### 03:25 — How it works

**VO:**
> Ippudu product lo rendu bhagalu.
> **Practice:** 12 games, rendu company patterns, communication rounds mee voice tho score avuthayi. ₹299 premium, oka saari. List price ₹500.
> **Connect:** meeru senior ni choose chesukuntaru, slot pick chestaru, pay chestaru. Memu manually confirm chesthamu, iddariki okate meeting link pampisthamu.
> Manual enduku? Enduku ante wrong slot vachina damage kanna, manual ga confirm cheyyadam better. Adi feature kaadu — naa ops decision.

**Supers:** `PRACTICE — ₹299` · `CONNECT — PER BOOKING` · `MANUAL ON PURPOSE`
**Visual:** Real screen recording, mouse visible, no fancy transitions. This is the "show your work" chapter — Starter Story always has one.

---

### 04:00 — The stack

**VO:**
> Stack simple ga undi, enduku ante nenu okkadine.
> React, TypeScript, Vite. Tailwind. Clerk for auth. Supabase — Postgres, RLS, 28 migrations, 24 edge functions. Razorpay. Resend for email. Azure Speech — speaking rounds score cheyyadam kosam. Backend FastAPI on AWS Lambda. Frontend Vercel.

**Visual:** **Stack wall** — logo grid, same as a Starter Story stack section. Clerk, Supabase, Razorpay, Azure, React, Vite, Tailwind, FastAPI, AWS, Vercel, Resend. One row in, one row out.

---

### 04:25 — Costs and margins

**VO:**
> Costs chala takkuva. Vercel free tier, Supabase free tier, Azure Speech free tier.
> Razorpay transaction ki approximately 2% teesukuntundi. So ₹299 lo naaku approximately ₹293 migulutundi.
> Connect lo senior price fix chestaru, memu 10% platform fee teesukuntamu.
> Naa actual monthly cost ippudu [ACTUAL ₹]. Ee video lo revenue number ledu — enduku ante inka peddaga ledu. Kani cost number undi, adi nijam.

**Supers:** `₹299 → ₹293` · `PLATFORM FEE 10%` · `MONTHLY COST ₹[N]`
**Visual:** Plain table on white. Numbers only. No animation.

---

### 04:45 — What I'd do differently

**VO:**
> Moodu vishayalu.
> Okati: nenu first version ni friends ki chupinchadam late chesanu. Rendu nelalu build chesi taruvata chupinchanu. Aa rendu nelalu waste.
> Rendu: nenu premium price ni mundhe fix chesa. ₹299 ani cheppadam valla product cheap ga anipinchindi. Kani adi nijam — naa students budget adi. Aa confusion ni nenu solve cheyyaledu.
> Moodu: nenu Connect ni first nunchi build cheyyalsindi. Software okkate chalu ani anukunnanu. Adi naa biggest mistake.

**Super:** `3 THINGS I'D CHANGE`
**Visual:** Numbered card per point. Static. This is the most-saved chapter — make it readable, not pretty.

---

### 05:05 — The final word

**VO:**
> Software round ni nerpistundi. Room ni nerpinchaledu.
> Ee rendu kalipi — adi ne naa product.
> Meeru kuda edaina build chesthunna, ee line gurthu pettukondi: meeru automate cheyyalenidi, meeru charge cheyyalsinadi.

**Supers:** `harrytheblaze.site` · `Free account. Premium ₹299.`
**Visual:** Back to camera, same desk as the intro. Full circle, no music swell.

## Numbers: sourced vs. yours

**From your repo — safe to say on camera:**
| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| 343 commits | `git rev-list --count HEAD` |
| 11 months (~Nov 2025 → Oct 2026) | first and last commit dates |
| 28 migrations, 24 edge functions | `supabase/` |
| Stack list | `README.md` |

**Yours to fill — I can't know these:**
1. `[BRANCH]` `[COLLEGE]` `[N]` students
2. **`[WHAT BROKE]` — one specific bug.** The single most important sentence in the script.
3. `[N]` sent it, `[N]` actually sat the round
4. `[ACTUAL ₹]` monthly cost — even ₹400 is fine; a real number beats a big one
5. Verify the 11 months. Git says Nov 2025. If you built an earlier version before that, say the real timeline — the commit count stays true either way.

**Never say:** revenue you don't have, a placement rate, an Accenture partnership, or a student quote with a name. `PRODUCT.md` rules all four out.

## Delivery notes

**Talk, don't present.** Starter Story works because the founder sounds like they're explaining it to one person who's about to try the same thing. If you read this like a script, it dies.

**Numbers get said flat.** "343 commits" — no emphasis, no pause before it. The confidence is in not selling the number.

**Two places to slow down:** the friend's line at 02:20, and "devenini nenu automate cheyyaledu" at 02:55. Those are the only two emotional beats. Everything else is information.

**Camera:** one setup, one lens, white wall, window light. No b-roll of a laptop being opened thoughtfully. This format earns trust by looking unproduced.

**Chapters must be real.** If you cut a chapter, cut it from the map too — a card that promises "costs and margins" and then skips numbers is the fastest way to lose the audience this format attracts.
