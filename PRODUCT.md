# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary user: 2026 campus / final-year engineering students targeting Accenture and similar MNCs. They are preparing for cognitive games and communication rounds, often with an interview date already on the calendar.

Secondary users (not this UI pass): recently placed seniors who list as Connect experts; the operator who manually schedules paid 1:1 calls.

## Product Purpose

Harry The Blaze is a placement-prep product. Students practice company-style cognitive games and communication rounds, then book a paid live 1:1 with a recently placed senior. Success is a student who practices the real round types and, when ready, books a coordinated call.

## Positioning

Practice the exact Accenture-style cognitive and communication rounds here, then talk to someone who just got placed — not a generic career coach. Connect is operated manually: pay first, the Connect team confirms the slot and sends one meeting link to both sides.

## Operating Context

Students arrive from landing or dashboard, often on a phone. They pick a company (Accenture is the current campaign), play games, or open Connect to choose a senior and a 20-minute slot. Payment is Razorpay. Premium unlocks the practice suite. Connect 1:1 is a separate paid booking.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Premium practice: cognitive games and communication rounds, currently priced ₹299 against a ₹500 list with a same-day offer timer
- Connect 1:1: Clerk-signed-in checkout, server-priced order, webhook recovery, admin-sent meeting link
- Radar job match and company dashboard exist
- Do not invent placement rates, company partnerships, or testimonials
- Connect is in-app (`/connect`), not an external booking link

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Harry The Blaze
- User-pinned: do not use generic “AI colors” (purple gradients, amber glow, neon accents)
- Selling surfaces must present both offers: games get students in; Connect 1:1 is the featured live offer
- Voice: direct, campus-plain, no hype words that sound like a SaaS template

## Evidence on Hand

- Real product surfaces: landing, dashboard, Connect booking, games
- No approved customer quotes or official Accenture partnership mark. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the round the student is actually facing (cognitive + communication), not with a feature grid
- Make Connect 1:1 feel like a real senior call, not a marketplace tile
- Keep money and next action obvious: practice now, or book a 20-minute call
- Never claim a placement outcome the product cannot prove
- Preserve the manual Connect ops truth in student-facing copy
